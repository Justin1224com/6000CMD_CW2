MediVault PDPA 2010 Data Privacy Policy

      

        
1. Notice & Choice Principle (Malaysia PDPA 2010): MediVault collects your full name, email, Malaysian phone number, and NRIC strictly for healthcare appointments and issuing encrypted e-prescriptions.

        
2. Cryptographic Data Protection: Firebase Authentication manages your password. Prescriptions and their NRIC fields use AES-256-GCM with random keys and IVs. Personal profiles and appointments are protected by database access rules and Firestore storage encryption; they are not separately encrypted by this application. Prescription keys are kept in separate restricted documents. The Firebase project owner remains a trusted operator.

        
3. Access Control (RBAC & PoLP): Your clinical records are available to you and the assigned doctor. Clinic administrators manage roles and inspect ciphertext and activity logs.

        
4. Choice and retention: Contact the project administrator to request access, correction or deletion of your test data. Retain coursework data only until marking is complete, then arrange deletion with the project owner.

        
5. Safe use: Use fictional medical and identity data for this coursework. Use a unique password, protect email links, keep your browser updated, and sign out on shared devices.
